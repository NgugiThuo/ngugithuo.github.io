Bio

I'm a spatial data scientist based in Nairobi, Kenya, with a BSc in Geospatial Information Science and Remote Sensing from Dedan Kimathi University of Technology. My work sits at the intersection of geospatial engineering, environmental science, and data infrastructure.

I currently work at Flux Carbon, a climate startup where I support Enhanced Rock Weathering (ERW) carbon removal initiatives through geospatial data science. This involves designing spatial sampling strategies, automating MRV workflows, managing geodatabases, and deploying data services on AWS work that directly contributes to quantifying and verifying carbon removal at scale.

I am proficient across both the Esri ecosystem ArcGIS Pro, ArcGIS Online, Field Maps, Dashboards and open source tools including QGIS, GeoPandas, PostGIS, and R. I believe good geospatial work lives at the junction of rigorous analysis and clear communication.

I also volunteer as a mapper with the Humanitarian OpenStreetMap Team (HOT), contributing open geographic data to support disaster response and sustainable development globally.
